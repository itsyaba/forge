# Surface brief: Progress + Profile tabs

Mode: Operate. Inside the established Style A world (near-black, neon lime, Anton/Archivo). The owner rejected v1: “boring card stack”, “feels empty / demotivating”, and “profile is a wall of chips”. The owner chose two things: Progress leads with *this week + streak*, and Profile is an *athlete card + settings rows*.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each tab opens on one lime poster panel that answers its question (“how’s my week?”, “who am I training as?”). Below it, content flows on the black ground in typographic sections divided by hairlines and space, never by stacked grey cards. This replaces the dashboard card stack.

OWN-WORLD: The lime panel (#D4FF3A, black Anton) is the only filled block on each screen. Everything else sits on #0B0B0B: Anton section headings, Archivo rows and hairlines (#262626), and lime used only for numbers that went up and for selection.

STORY:
- Progress: this week’s tally as seven tall day columns plus a plain-words headline (“1 DOWN. 3 TO GO.”), then the all-time poster tally, getting stronger, the last 12 weeks, and body weight.
- Profile: the athlete card (initial, name, facts, split), then grouped setting rows that open a bottom sheet editor.

TONE: Encouraging, never judging. No bare “0”; empty states say what will appear and how to get it.

SIGNATURE: The lime week panel, with day columns that fill black as workouts land.

RISKS: The hero panel becoming a hero-metric template (keep it a sentence, not a number). Settings rows going grey and generic (keep Anton values and lime selection in the sheets).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Progress shows the title, then the lime week panel (a sentence headline, 7 day columns, the streak line), then the start of the all-time tally. Profile shows the lime athlete card (initial, name, facts line, split row, record line), then the first Training rows.

FORM: The owner pinned both structures in the AskUserQuestion round (Progress leads with this week + streak; Profile is an athlete card + settings), so no concept roll was run. seed: none (owner-pinned).

TRUTH RULE (from review): the week panel never promises a target the remaining days can’t reach, and days before the account existed are neutral, never “missed”.
